Welcome to MARCO LAB

Reliable Laboratory Reagents, Equipment and Purified Water — Manufactured and Supplied in Sri Lanka

MARCO LAB (PVT) LTD is a Sri Lankan manufacturer and supplier of medical laboratory reagents, laboratory equipment, distilled water and highly purified water. Based in Kuruvita, we support hospitals, diagnostic laboratories, universities, research institutions and other scientific facilities with practical laboratory solutions.
